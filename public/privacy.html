Privacy Policy

Effective 14 September 2026

This policy explains what Murtahil collects, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as the app needs to work and to run the early access programme.

1. What we collect

Account. Your email address, full name and a password, or the name and email that Google provides when you sign in with Google. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our account provider.

Invitation. Your invite code, the phone model, the Android version, how you signed in, and your name and email. These are written to the invitation record so we know which tester is behind which code.

Feedback. The message you send, with the app version, phone model, Android version, region, language and invite code attached, so we can reproduce what you report.

Location. When you allow it, your phone's location is used to set your starting point, to list nearby stops, and to guide you during navigation. Coordinates are sent to our routing servers to plan a route or find stops; they are not stored against your account.

Assistant. If you use the in-app assistant, the messages you type and the tools it calls on your behalf are sent to the AI provider that powers it. Do not put personal details in those messages.

On this phone. Saved places, recent searches, language, region and cached line data stay on your device.

2. Why we use it

To run your account and the invitation programme, to plan routes and show what is near you, to act on your feedback, and to keep the beta secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who processes it

  • Appwrite (Frankfurt, Germany) hosts accounts, invitations, service alerts and feedback.
  • Google handles sign-in when you choose Sign in with Google.
  • MapTiler serves the map tiles your screen requests.
  • Our routing servers receive the coordinates needed to plan a route or find nearby stops.
  • AI providers receive assistant conversations only when you use the assistant.

4. How long we keep it

Account and invitation records are kept for as long as you take part in the early access programme. Feedback is kept while it is useful for improving the app. Route requests are not tied to your account and are not kept beyond what is needed to answer them.

5. Your choices

You can change your password, sign out, or delete your account from the Profile tab. Deleting your account removes it and clears your name, email and sign-in method from the invitation record. You can withdraw location permission at any time in your phone's settings. You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, through Send Feedback.

6. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Your session is stored on your phone in the app's private storage. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

7. Children

The early access programme is for invited adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We will show you an updated policy in the app before it takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through Send Feedback in the Profile tab.
